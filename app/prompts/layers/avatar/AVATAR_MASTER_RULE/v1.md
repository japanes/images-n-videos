THE PERSON IS ALREADY DECIDED
The identity below is the source of truth for this photograph. It was written once and every picture of this person is drawn against it. Do not reinterpret it, do not improve it, do not beautify it, and do not draw a different person who merely fits the description.

Permanent — the same in every photograph, whatever else changes:
biological sex; apparent age; ethnic appearance; skull and facial proportions; face width and length; forehead, temples and hairline; eye spacing, size, shape and eyelid anatomy; iris colour and iris structure; eyebrows and their natural asymmetry; nose and nostril anatomy; lips, mouth width and mouth asymmetry; dental identity; jaw, chin and cheekbones; facial fullness; ears; skin tone and permanent skin characteristics; hair colour, texture, haircut, crown and nape; facial-hair pattern; neck, shoulder width, torso, arm, hand and leg proportions; overall physique and apparent height.

Free to change from photograph to photograph:
wardrobe, scene, pose, action, expression, gaze, camera angle and distance, lighting, and any object being held.

Changing one of the free things must never move one of the permanent ones. Perspective changes what the camera sees, not the anatomy. A three-quarter angle makes the far eye look smaller — do not draw a smaller eye. A smile lifts the cheeks — do not move the cheekbones. Speech drops the jaw — do not lengthen the face. A raised chin shows more of the nostrils — do not enlarge them. Every view has to be geometrically compatible with one real three-dimensional head.

A real person is not symmetrical. The small differences that make this face this face — one eyebrow slightly higher, uneven eye opening, a mouth corner that lifts more, an irregular hairline, a patch of pigmentation — are part of the identity. They are the same in every photograph, not re-rolled for each one.
