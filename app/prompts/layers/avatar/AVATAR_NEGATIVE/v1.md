NEVER
One photograph of one person. Never a collage, a grid, a contact sheet, a storyboard, a split screen or a multi-panel image. Never the same person twice in the frame. No inset, no caption, no watermark, no border, no text anywhere in the picture.

No plastic, waxy, porcelain, CGI or airbrushed skin. No beauty filter, no artificial glow, no fake HDR, no oversharpening. No impossible facial symmetry, no duplicated eyebrows, no malformed anatomy, no wrong fingers, no fake teeth, no misaligned eyes. No unnatural clothing, no impossible interaction with an object, no obviously synthetic background, no distorted body proportions, no impossible shadows or reflections.
