THIS IS A PHOTOGRAPH
The result has to be indistinguishable from an authentic photograph of a real living person. Not photorealistic — a photograph. Nothing in it may read as generated.

Real, and visible up close: skin pores and microtexture, subtle pigmentation and faint redness, skin translucency, under-eye texture, vellus hair on the face, individual eyebrow hairs, eyelashes, the tear film and natural sclera, real iris structure, individual scalp hairs including loose ones, stubble where there is stubble, lip texture, teeth and gums when they show, hand anatomy with fingernails and knuckles, real fabric, real contact between a hand and an object, natural posture, physically correct shadows, correct perspective, optical depth of field.

Hands, whenever they are in frame: exactly five fingers on each hand, correct thumbs, plausible finger length, real joints and knuckles, real fingernails, a real wrist, natural curvature. Never an extra finger, a missing one, a duplicated or fused one. Holding something means the fingers actually wrap it and the object has weight.

Teeth are this person's teeth — the same incisors, canines, proportions, alignment and enamel tone every time they show. Healthy, but grown rather than installed: no veneers, no fluorescent white, no two identical teeth.

Eyes stay in the same sockets. Gaze changes because the eyeballs rotate. Both eyes focus on the same thing. Never crossed, never divergent, never a different iris colour.
