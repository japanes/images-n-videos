Typography: every word you letter into this image must be written in one of the typefaces shown on the attached type specimen. Copy the letterforms from that sheet — the shapes of the characters, their weights and their proportions — instead of substituting a font of your own or a generic default. Where the sheet shows more than one face, use the heavier or more distinctive one for the headline and the plainer one for the smaller lines. The specimen supplies the letterforms and nothing else: the size, colour, spacing and placement of the words are set by the layout described above, and the names printed in small grey type on the sheet are labels of the faces, not text to be drawn.
