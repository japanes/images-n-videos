=== CAPTIONS TO REWRITE ===
Every caption below is painted onto a picture and read at a glance, not as prose. Rewrite each one and return them under the same markers, in the same order, with nothing else in the answer.

Rules for every caption:
- Keep it about as long as the original: it has to fit the same spot on the picture.
- Keep the line breaks. Each line is laid out on its own.
- Never end a line with a full stop or an ellipsis. A question or an exclamation mark is fine.
- Keep the facts: every number, name, price and date stays exactly as it is.
- A caption that is already plain human speech comes back unchanged.
